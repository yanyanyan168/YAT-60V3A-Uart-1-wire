3、功能说明 Function Specification

3.1 LED指示灯说明

  • 3.11 指示灯: 红绿指示灯各一个。Indicating led: a red led, a green led.

3.2 充电逻辑 Charging principle

  • 3.21 当充电器插入市电时，红灯指示灯常亮，绿灯熄灭；当电池包插入时，通过电压判断电池包是否插入。 When the charger is applied to the power supplier, red led is on and green led is off; Check whether battery pack is plugged in through voltage.
  • 3.22 当电池包电压低于 1*NV 时，关断输出，红灯闪烁。 When the battery pack voltage is below 1*NV, cut off the output, the charger will go into abnormal state with red led blinking.
  • 3.23 当电池电压在 1 - 2.5V 每节时，以预充电流充电，此时红灯熄灭，绿灯闪烁；如果 30 分钟无法充到 2.5V 每节以上，进入异常状态：绿灯熄灭，红灯闪烁。 When battery's voltage is between 1*N - 2.5*N V, a current of Pre charge is used for charging with red led off and green led blinking; If battery's voltage can't reach above 2.5*N V after 30min charging, the charger will go into abnormal state with green led off and red led blinking.
  • 3.24 当电池电压大于 2.5V 每节时，进入到大电流 CC 充电，此时红灯熄灭，绿灯闪烁。 When battery's voltage is more than 2.5*N V, a current of CC is used for charging with red led off and green led blinking.
  • 3.25 当检测到电池电压充到设定电压 4.20*N V，逐渐减少充电电流，此时红灯熄灭，绿灯闪烁；当电流减少到关断电流时，指示灯指示充满，完全切断充电。 When battery's voltage reaches 4.20*N V, charging current will be reduced gradually with red lamp off and green lamp blinking; Until the charging current is reduced to end current, cut off the output.
  • 3.26 & 3.27 温度与异常恢复逻辑: 当进入到温度异常状态时，温度恢复后可自行充电；当进入到电流、电压或通信异常状态，需重新插拔电池后才能再次开启检测。 After the temperature recover from abnormal value, the charger will automatically start charging again. If the current or voltage or communication is abnormal, the battery pack has to be plugged out and in to operate again.
